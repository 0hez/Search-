New tab works for every site. The in-app viewer shows a desktop layout shrunk to your screen, but many sites (Google included) block being shown inside other pages.

Fit any site (works everywhere)

A one-tap bookmark that fixes the page you’re on. It leaves mobile-friendly sites alone and only shrinks pages that are wider than your screen.
  1. Tap Copy, then bookmark any page in your browser and name it “Fit”.
  2. Edit that bookmark and paste the code as its address/URL.
  3. On any desktop-only site, open your bookmarks and tap “Fit”.
Android Chrome: you can also type “Fit” in the address bar and pick the bookmark. Tip: if a site still looks tiny, run it once more to re-measure.
If the page stays blank, the site blocks embedding. Tap “Open tab” and use the Fit bookmark there.
